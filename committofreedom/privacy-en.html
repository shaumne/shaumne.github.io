Privacy Policy: Commit to Freedom

Last updated: 26 October 2026

1. Who we are

Commit to Freedom (the "game") is made and published by Shaumne Games, a one-person studio. This policy explains what happens to data when you play, and why.

Contact: shaumnegames@gmail.com

2. The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, address or birth date.
  • Your progress, your choices and the name you give your AI stay on your phone.
  • To fix crashes and see where players get stuck, the game sends anonymous usage data and crash reports (Google Firebase). You can switch them off at any time: Settings → More → Usage data.
  • Ads appear only when you tap a "watch an ad" button yourself. Where the law asks for it (EEA, UK, Switzerland) you are asked for consent first, and you can change your answer in Settings → More → Ad choices.
  • Purchases go through Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell data. We do not build profiles of you.

3. Data that stays on your device

The save file on your phone holds your progress, the decisions you made in the story, the name you typed for your AI, the hero you picked and your settings (sound, language, accessibility, notifications, your usage-data choice). It is never sent to us. Deleting the game (or clearing its storage) deletes it.

4. Usage data (Google Analytics for Firebase)

In the store version of the game, Google Analytics for Firebase records what happens in the game, so we can see where the first fifteen minutes lose players, how long the story chapters take and whether an update broke something. It is on by default and you can turn it off in Settings → More → Usage data. While it is off, nothing is sent.

What the game itself sends: event names with numbers and identifiers from the game, for example "phase 2 reached after 40 minutes", "first job finished", "card evil_offer_1 answered", "ending seen", "a rewarded ad was offered, watched or skipped", "store opened", "the no-ads pack was bought", "screen opened: skill tree". The words you type (such as your AI's name) and any text shown on screen are never sent. Prices and amounts are never sent.

What Firebase adds by itself: a random app-instance identifier, the device model, operating system and app version, the device language, an approximate country derived from the network address, and standard events such as first open and session start. The game switches off the collection of advertising identifiers for analytics, so usage data is not linked to your advertising ID.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the game crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a report: which part of the code failed, the device model, operating system and app version, free memory and disk space, and a random identifier for this installation used to count how many devices are affected. Reports are not linked to you and are not used for advertising. The same switch controls them: with usage data off, no crash report leaves your phone.

6. Game settings from the network (Firebase Remote Config)

When the game starts, it asks Firebase Remote Config whether the rewarded-ad settings have changed (for example how many times a day a button may appear). To answer, Firebase uses a random installation identifier and the app version. This is only used to deliver those settings, not to analyse or advertise, and it happens without any account.

7. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game has rewarded ads only: no banners, no ads that pop up between screens. An ad plays only after you tap a button that says it will, next to the timer it affects. Ads speed up time and resources; they never change the story.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier, network address (IP), device information and how you interacted with the ad.

  • EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland: before any ad is requested, Google's consent form asks for your choice. You can change it at any time in Settings → More → Ad choices. If consent is needed and you do not give it, the game requests no ads at all; every ad button simply stays hidden and the game plays the same.
  • iPhone and iPad: the system asks whether the game may track you across apps (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, ads are not personalised and nothing else changes.
  • No-ads pack: if you buy it, no ad is ever shown; the buttons give their reward straight away.

Google's information on ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

8. Purchases

The no-ads pack and the optional tips are bought through Google Play or the App Store. Your payment details go to the store only; we never see or store them. From the store the game learns only whether a product was bought, so that it can unlock it on this device and restore it on a new one. There is no purchase server of ours. Tips give nothing in the game, and there are no loot boxes or random paid rewards.

9. Notifications

If you allow them, the game sends up to two reminders a day (never at night), for example when your AI has finished work while you were away. They are scheduled on your phone, not sent from a server, and no notification token is collected. Turn them off in Settings → General or in your phone's settings.

10. Optional cloud save (Google Play Games, Game Center)

Where it is available, you can turn on cloud save in Settings. A copy of your save file (the same data as in section 3, including your AI's name) is then stored in your own Google Play Games saved games (Android) or Game Center / iCloud (iPhone, iPad), under Google's or Apple's terms. You sign in with their service; we do not receive your Google or Apple account details and we cannot read the copy. Turn it off in the same place; you can delete the copy in the Play Games app or in your iCloud settings. If you never turn it on, nothing is stored there.

11. Sharing your card

The game can make a picture of your run (your AI, a few numbers from your playthrough). It is created on your phone and shared only if you tap Share and pick an app in your phone's share sheet. We receive nothing; the app you share to handles it under its own policy.

12. What we never collect

No account, name, email, phone number, address, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera data, and nothing you type is sent anywhere. The game has no chat with other players.

14. Who receives data

Only the service providers named above, and only for the purposes described: Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, AdMob, Google Play, Google Play Games) and Apple (App Store, Game Center, iCloud). Their servers may be outside your country (for example in the United States); they protect such transfers with the safeguards the law requires, such as standard contractual clauses. We do not sell or rent data to anyone.

15. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete the game or its data.
  • Usage data: deleted automatically after 14 months at the latest.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Ad data: as described in Google's policies.
  • Cloud save: until you delete it in Play Games or iCloud.

16. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK) you may ask to access, correct or delete data, object to processing, receive a copy, or withdraw consent, and you may complain to your data protection authority (in Turkey: the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

  • Stop usage data and crash reports: Settings → More → Usage data.
  • Change your ad choice: Settings → More → Ad choices.
  • Delete everything on the device: delete the game.
  • Anything else: write to shaumnegames@gmail.com. Because the game has no account, we cannot tell which data is yours unless you help us find it; we answer within 30 days and do what we can.

17. Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. It does not ask for your age and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and data should be deleted, write to shaumnegames@gmail.com and we will help.

18. Security

Everything the game sends travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to the providers above. The save file on your phone stays in the game's private storage.

19. Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date changes too. Important changes are announced in the game.

20. Contact
